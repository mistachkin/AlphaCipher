# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) version 2.1.

The canonical text is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html .

In short:

- Be respectful of other contributors and users.
- Assume good faith when reading and replying to messages.
- Focus on the technical merits of a proposal, not on the person proposing it.
- Disagreements are welcome; personal attacks are not.

## Enforcement

Use the same private reporting channel as security reports
(`Report a vulnerability` under the *Security* tab — see
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)).  Reports are reviewed by the
maintainers and treated confidentially.
